# Audit of Previous Generated Review

## Major corrections required

1. Tangviriyapaiboon et al. (2022): previous review reported sensitivity 90.9% and specificity 92.5%. PDF text reports sensitivity 100% and specificity 82.4%.
2. Srisinghasongkram et al. (2016): previous review reported N=292 with 142/150 split. PDF abstract reports high-risk language-delay children N=109 and low-risk TD children N=732.
3. Mohanta et al. (2020): previous review described Random Forest/SVM with 88.5% average accuracy. PDF reports KNN as highest at 96.5%; Random Forest was not the central reported result found in the paper text.
4. Yin et al. (2026): previous review reported N=124, CNN+Random Forest, sensitivity 83.1%, specificity 87.2%. PDF reports 88 infants, 28 ASD, best sigmoid-kernel SVM with sensitivity 92.86%, specificity 93.33%, accuracy 93.18%.
5. Georgiou & Paphiti (2026): previous review reported N=50 and 91.2%. PDF/preprint text reports 18 autistic adults + 18 neurotypical adults and best discriminability about 89%. It is a medRxiv preprint and should be Weak/future-only evidence.
6. Quality distribution was inflated. A defensible quality profile for the corrected final set is 7 Strong, 12 Moderate, and 2 Weak among 21 final papers, not 15/18 Strong.
