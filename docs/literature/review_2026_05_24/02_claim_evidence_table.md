# Claim-Evidence Table

|Claim|Evidence|Strength|Caveat|
|---|---|---|---|
|Speech/acoustic markers can support ASD screening, but performance is task- and corpus-dependent.|Ma et al. (2024); Rybner et al. (2022); Mohanta et al. (2020); Yin et al. (2026); Chi et al. (2022)|Moderate to strong|Mostly controlled/small datasets; external validation is the central unresolved issue.|
|Transcript/NLP approaches are highly aligned with asd-project because they use interpretable linguistic markers such as MLU/turn ratio.|Assaf et al. (2025); Themistocleous et al. (2024)|Moderate|They depend on accurate transcription and language-specific feature validity.|
|Thai ASD screening/local context must not be treated as interchangeable with English or Western corpora.|Tangviriyapaiboon et al. (2022, 2024); Srisinghasongkram et al. (2016); Chaturavitwong (2023)|Moderate|Thai evidence is mostly clinical-scale/background evidence, not Thai speech-AI validation.|
|A safe ASD-AI tool should include uncertainty/indeterminate handling instead of forced binary decisions.|Megerian et al. (2022); Bae et al. (2025); Eni et al. (2025)|Strong|Indeterminate outputs reduce automation but improve safety and clinical realism.|
|Fairness and subgroup bias need to be explicit evaluation targets.|Al Futaisi et al. (2025); Rakotomanana & Rouhafzay (2025); Rybner et al. (2022)|Moderate|Few studies provide robust subgroup validation, especially for Thai/tonal-language settings.|
