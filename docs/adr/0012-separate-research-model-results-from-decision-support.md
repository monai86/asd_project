# Separate research model results from therapist-facing decision support

Model training and evaluation results belong to the research and presentation context, while the therapist app may only show cautious decision-support outputs such as review priority, concern level, or reviewed reference cohort similarity. This separation prevents public-corpus performance metrics from being mistaken for clinical validation of the therapist workflow, especially because the prototype is not validated for Thai children and all transcript-derived interpretation requires therapist review.
