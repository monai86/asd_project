# Focus on progress tracking and bilingual clinical reporting with human overrides

To ensure safety and alignment with clinician workflows, the final prototype and term-paper analysis will focus on **Progress Tracking & Audio-to-Report** rather than diagnostic screening. The progress report will use a **Bilingual format** with a templated **Safe Thai Summary** that translates key developmental trends (MLU, TTR, and repeating speech behaviors) into descriptive Thai.

To respect the clinical role and enforce a **Human-in-the-Loop** model, the frontend will present this summary as an editable pre-populated template, allowing the therapist to modify or override the summary before downloading as Markdown or printing to PDF. The custom summary will be saved to the case state and reports collections, ensuring data persistence and safety.
